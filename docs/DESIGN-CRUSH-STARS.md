# Two stars for a squash

James cannot rely on the written reward banner. Keep the toy visibly flattening and add two clear little stars rising from that actual car when a successful crush earns its existing two-star reward. This is visual feedback only; the simulation remains the sole owner of contacts, scores and turbo charge.

RoadEncounters already sees each new crushed-car ID. On its first transition, capture the car's world frame and show two original extruded star silhouettes for approximately 0.8 seconds. They rise and separate slightly above the flattened toy behind the departing truck, then shrink away. Their source stays with the car, so they cannot become another pickup that the player must chase. Do not add text, controls, sound or timers. Existing small reward flecks can be reduced only after the new cue is judged in real driving.

Use one owned InstancedMesh, capacity two, with one low-cost geometry/material. Target under 200 submitted triangles and one draw; no cast shadow or global bloom. A later simultaneous crush may replace the previous cue. Repeated updates of an already crushed ID must not retrigger it, including after gentler motion is switched off. Reset hides/reuses the effect; disposal releases each owned resource exactly once. Running updates cap positive dt; paused and invalid-time frames freeze all state and buffers. Gentler motion suppresses the decorative star rise while retaining the flattened car and earned rewards. Menu hides the effect.

Acceptance uses real touch/keyboard play at the first car and a bay car with the smallest and largest player models, landscape and portrait. Watch the complete transient: stars must emerge into view as the truck leaves, stay recognizable briefly, and fade without covering controls. Verify one trigger per car per race, no extra rewards, clean replay, pause and gentler behavior, fixed graphics resources, and integrated render cost. Keep the already published crew version available during this work.
