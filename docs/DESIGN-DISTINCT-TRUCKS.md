# A garage of different shapes

James's parent observes that the current trucks share too much of the same pickup shape. This increment adds two original vehicles whose identities come from their complete bodies: Rescue Roarer is a red monster fire engine, and Shark Surge has a rounded shark body, pale jaw, friendly teeth, fins and a tail. Their key details must remain recognizable from the chase camera and in the garage. Mega Titan should show a larger tire-to-body proportion and more exposed suspension.

Preserve the six existing IDs and their unlock thresholds. Add Rescue Roarer at 40 stars, scale 1.25; add Shark Surge at 48 stars, scale 1.3. Keep Mega Titan as the largest, final 100-star unlock. Version 1 saves and selected trucks survive unchanged; an existing earned balance may immediately qualify for the new trucks. No purchase, account, new driving requirement or separate progression currency.

The two new models replace the generic pickup shell with their own body geometry. Share the proven wheel/guardian animation contracts, chassis, exhaust anchors and material batching. Preserve the tested physical envelope and course clearance; make the Titan's tire proportion read larger by exposing its mechanical structure rather than silently widening collision assumptions. Use original forms and steady fire-engine lights, with no strobe effect.

Root owns the catalog, save compatibility checks, garage counts and portraits, scene integration, browser checks and publication. The model author owns models.mjs and focused model tests. Verify all eight models under normal and guardian articulation, loop/road clearance, passing, finite geometry, resource lifetime and the existing rendering budget. Render new portraits from the actual game models, then inspect both new vehicles in landscape/portrait driving views and a complete race.

Retain the current Three.js game for this increment. App Store research evaluates guided 3D and side-view presentation separately from ease of play. A 2D or 2.5D mode remains a possible later direction; an engine rewrite is not required to correct repetitive silhouettes here.
